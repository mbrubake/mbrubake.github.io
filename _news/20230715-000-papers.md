Happy to announce the following papers accepted at [ICCV 2023](https://iccv2023.thecvf.com/):
 - [_Reference-guided Controllable Inpainting of Neural Radiance Fields_](https://ashmrz.github.io/reference-guided-3d/) by Ashkan Mirzaei, Tristan Aumentado-Armstrong, Marcus A. Brubaker,  Jonathan Kelly, Igor Gilitschenski Alex Levinshtein, and Konstantinos G. Derpanis.
 - [_Long-Term Photometric Consistent Novel View Synthesis with Diffusion Models_](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.10700) by Jason Yu, Fereshteh Forghani, Konstantinos G. Derpanis and Marcus A. Brubaker.

Congratulations and thanks to all my wonderful collaborators on these projects.
