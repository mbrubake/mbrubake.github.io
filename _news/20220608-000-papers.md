The following new papers are now available:
 - [_Noise2NoiseFlow: Realistic Camera Noise Modeling without Clean Images_](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.01103) by Ali Maleky, Shayan Kousha, Michael S Brown, Marcus A Brubaker at CVPR 2022.
 - [_Modeling sRGB Camera Noise with Normalizing Flows_](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.0812) by Shayan Kousha, Ali Maleky, Michael S Brown, Marcus A Brubaker at CVPR 2022.
 - [_Learning sRGB-to-Raw De-rendering with Content-Aware Metadata_](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.01813) by Seonghyeon Nam and Abhijith Punnappurath and Marcus A. Brubaker and Michael S. Brown at CVPR 2022.
 - [_Residual Multiplicative Filter Networks for Multiscale Reconstruction_](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.00746) by Shayan Shekarforoush, David B Lindell, David J Fleet, Marcus A Brubaker on arXiv.
 - [_Efficient CDF Approximations for Normalizing Flows_](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.11322) by Chandramouli Shama Sastry, Andreas Lehrmann, Marcus Brubaker, Alexander Radovic on arXiv.

Congratulations and thanks to my fantastic co-authors!
