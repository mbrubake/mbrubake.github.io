I'm very happy to announce that my lab has been awarded a [Canada Foundation for Innovation](https://innovation.ca) grant as part of the [John R. Evans Leaders Fund](https://www.innovation.ca/about/press-release/government-canada-invests-more-330-leading-edge-research-projects-universities).  The grant, valued at $140,000CAD, will be used to purchase hardware to continue my groups research on generative models with a focus on applications in cryo-EM, hyperspectral imagery and video analysis.
